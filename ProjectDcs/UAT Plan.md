## 1. UAT Scenarios

### UAT-DOC-UI-001: Employee Signs Contract

**Steps**:
1. Login as Employee
2. Navigate to My Documents
3. Find "Employment Contract"
4. Click to view
5. Review contract
6. Click "Sign"
7. Draw signature
8. Submit
9. View confirmation

**Expected**: Document signed, status updated

---

### UAT-DOC-UI-002: HR Uploads Policy

**Steps**:
1. Login as HR Admin
2. Navigate to HR Documents
3. Click "Upload"
4. Select employee
5. Select "Policy Acknowledgment"
6. Upload PDF
7. Set 1 year expiry
8. Submit
9. Employee notified

**Expected**: Document uploaded and linked
